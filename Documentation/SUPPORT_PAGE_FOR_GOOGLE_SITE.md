Support for Postmark Protocol

Welcome to the support page for Postmark Protocol, a postal route puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. If you have run into a problem, have a question about how something works, or just want to share feedback, we would like to hear from you.

How to Reach Us

Send an email to buistovanhl@icloud.com and describe what happened. We read every message and try to respond as quickly as we reasonably can, though as a small team we cannot promise a specific response time.

What to Include in Your Message

A short description of the problem or question is usually enough, but the following details help us help you faster. Your device model, such as iPhone or iPad and the generation if you know it. Your iOS version, found in the Settings app under General and then About. The level number if the issue happened during a specific delivery. Any steps that led up to the problem, so we can try to reproduce it.

Common Questions

Postmark Protocol works fully offline and does not require an account, so there is nothing to sign in to and no account recovery is needed. All of your progress is saved automatically on your own device as you play. If you want to start over, open Desk Settings from the Main Menu and choose Reset Progress. This clears your delivery progress and seals but keeps your music, sound, and haptic preferences. This action cannot be undone, so use it only when you are sure.

Privacy Questions

For questions about how the app handles information, please see our Privacy Policy page rather than the general support address above, or feel free to send those questions to the same email address.

Thank You

Thank you for playing Postmark Protocol. Your feedback helps make the game better.
